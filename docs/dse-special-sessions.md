# Verified exceptional trading sessions

The collected DSE archive contains Saturday trades on 17 and 24 May 2025, and 23 May 2026. These are explicit dataset calendar exceptions, not a blanket removal of weekend validation. All other Friday/Saturday dates still fail CSV validation.

The official DSE day-end archive contains these dates for the collected companies. Corroboration: BRAC EPL publishes its [17 May 2025 daily market update](https://www.bracepl.com/brokerage/assets/research/marketupdate/Daily_Market_Update_May_17_2025.pdf), [Bangladesh Pratidin reports the DSE announcement for both May 2025 Saturdays](https://en.bd-pratidin.com/economy/2025/05/12/36872), and [AB & Co publishes the 23 May 2026 trading notice](https://www.abcdsebroker.com/notice/view/58).
