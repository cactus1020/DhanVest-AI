How the scores work

Quality uses reported ROE and debt-to-equity. Value compares positive P/E ratios with the median of at least three companies in the same sector. Fundamentals require a reporting date within the last 180 days. Momentum describes the return over 60 trading sessions; liquidity uses average volume over 20 sessions.

Base weights: quality 32%, value 28%, momentum 25%, liquidity 15%. Missing factors remain unavailable; available weights are renormalized and shown on the card. Coverage measures available factor weight, not prediction accuracy. Scores are heuristic research measures and have no proven investment performance.

Bangla explanations describe the displayed scores. When configured, a server-side AI service explains the data; otherwise fixed factor rules provide the explanation. The method is disclosed with each result. No price forecasts or buy/sell recommendations are requested.
