Simulation rules

Orders fill at the server's latest available DSE last-traded price while the source reports the market open. Public feed delay may occur. Every buy and sell includes a simulated 0.25% fee. This is a practice fee, not an official broker tariff.

Whole shares only; no margin or short selling. Settlement is instant in this first version. Order-book depth, slippage, actual exchange settlement, dividends and corporate actions are not yet simulated. Therefore results can differ from real DSE execution. No wallet resets or cash deposits.
